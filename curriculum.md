## How We'll Work Together

Every lesson follows the same rhythm (~45–60 minutes):

1. **Review & Quiz** (10 min) — I quiz you on last lesson's material. Spaced repetition, no cramming.
2. **New Material** (20 min) — One focused topic, explained properly (pronunciation, grammar, usage, cultural notes).
3. **Drills** (10 min) — Pronunciation, tones, pattern practice. You say it, I correct it.
4. **Speaking Practice** (10 min) — Roleplay, conversation, or read-aloud. We speak Mandarin from Lesson 1.
5. **Homework** (assigned) — Short daily practice (10–20 min) until next lesson.

You can message me anytime between lessons to practice, ask questions, or drill. I keep track of your progress in `mandarin-progress.md`.

## The Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation: Pinyin & Survival Mandarin (Lessons 1–8, ~2 months)
**Goal:** Master pinyin completely (all 21 initials, all finals, 4 tones + neutral, tone sandhi). First ~150 characters. Handle greetings, introductions, numbers, ordering food, prices.
**Aligns with:** HSK 1

### Phase 2 — Elementary: Everyday Communication (Lessons 9–20, ~3 months)
**Goal:** Core grammar — 了, 吗/呢, measure words, 有, 是, time words, 比-sentences. ~500 characters total. Describe your day, family, hobbies, shopping, directions.
**Aligns with:** HSK 2

### Phase 3 — Lower-Intermediate: Building Fluency (Lessons 21–36, ~5 months)
**Goal:** Complex sentences — relative clauses, 被-sentences, 把-sentences, resultative & potential complements, 一边…一边, 越来越. ~1,000 characters. Discuss opinions, past experiences, future plans.
**Aligns with:** HSK 3

### Phase 4 — Intermediate: Real Conversations (Lessons 37–56, ~7 months)
**Goal:** Advanced structures — 趁, 无论…都, 就是…也, colloquial speech, 成语 (idioms) introduced. ~1,800 characters. Read short articles, follow simple shows, give short talks.
**Aligns with:** HSK 4

### Phase 5 — Upper-Intermediate (Lessons 57–76, ~8 months)
**Goal:** Nuance — synonyms, formal vs casual register, regional variation awareness, 成语/歇后语. ~2,500 characters. Watch native content, debate topics, write essays.
**Aligns with:** HSK 5

### Phase 6 — Advanced (ongoing)
**Goal:** 3,500+ characters, professional/academic register, HSK 6, optional Classical Chinese introduction.

**Estimated total journey at ~2–3 lessons/week: 2.5–3 years to advanced fluency.** We adjust pace to your life — there is no deadline.

## Pro-Level Study Principles

1. **Speak from day 1.** Even badly. Pronunciation is muscle memory, not knowledge.
2. **Shadow native audio daily.** 10 minutes of repeating-after-a-speaker beats an hour of silent study.
3. **Tones are sacred.** A wrong tone = a wrong word. Drill tones every single day.
4. **Learn characters with stroke order** — never just "shape memorize." It pays off at 500+ characters.
5. **Massive input, patient output.** Listen/read a lot; don't fear making mistakes when speaking.
6. **Consistency beats intensity.** 20 minutes daily > 3 hours once a week.
