## Why this first

Every Mandarin syllable is built on these six vowels. If a vowel is clean, every word containing it is easier. If one vowel is off, it contaminates every word you ever say with it. So the rule for this program is: **perfect, not fast.**

---

## The six vowels — with mouth cues

| # | Pinyin | IPA | English anchor | Mouth cue | Common mistake |
|---|--------|-----|----------------|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | **a** | [ä] | "ah" in **father** | Jaw drops wide, mouth open, tongue low and flat | Too small/tense — sounds like "uh" |
| 2 | **o** | [ɔ] | "or" in **more** | Lips rounded and pushed forward, tongue mid-back | Lips too loose — drifts toward "e" |
| 3 | **e** | [ɤ] | "er" in **her**, but flat | Tongue flat in the middle, **no curl**; lips slightly spread | Curling the tongue (English "r") |
| 4 | **i** | [i] | "ee" in **see** | Wide smile, tongue high and front, tip resting low | Too lax — sounds like "ih/uh" |
| 5 | **u** | [u] | "oo" in **boot** | Small, tight lip circle; tongue high and **back** | Lips too loose or spread |
| 6 | **ü** | [y] | French "tu" / German "ü" — **no English equivalent** | Say "ee", then round the lips tightly; tongue high and **front** | Tongue too far back (→ becomes u) or lips unrounded (→ becomes i) |

**Example words (zero initial):** 阿 ā · 哦 ó · 鹅 é · 衣 yī · 乌 wū · 鱼 yú

**Confusion pairs to drill:**
- **i vs ü** — same tongue (front), different lips (spread vs rounded)
- **u vs ü** — same lips (rounded), different tongue (back vs front)
- **o vs e** — the difference is lip rounding

> 💡 Pro note: after j/q/x/y, ü is written as plain **u** (ju, qu, xu, yu) — the sound is still ü. (Comes in a later lesson.)

## The 5-step protocol (use it EVERY practice session)

1. **LISTEN** — Play the shadow track 3×, eyes closed. Input before output.
2. **ANCHORS** — Say each English anchor word 5× (father, more, her, see, boot).
3. **SHADOW** — Play the track; repeat each sound immediately after it. 10 reps per vowel.
4. **RECORD** — Record yourself: the six vowels in sequence, then 阿 哦 鹅 衣 乌 鱼.
5. **CHECK** — Compare your recording against the mouth cues and anchors. Mark each vowel ✅ clean or 🔴 needs work.
